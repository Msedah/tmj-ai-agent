**Checked:** 2026-10-05. These notes support TMJ AI's local context and official source directory. They are not a live service-status feed.

## Sekororo, Metz and nearby facilities

- Maruleng Local Municipality's 2024/25 Integrated Development Plan places Maruleng in Mopani District, Limpopo. It names Metz as a Ward 9 settlement, a formal taxi rank at Ga-Sekororo (Masokisi), and one hospital at Sekororo. The IDP uses older census material for some settlement/ward context, so it is not a current boundary layer or proof that every nearby place is in a particular ward. [1]
- The Limpopo Department of Health lists **Sekororo hospital** under Mopani District at Sekororo, phone **015 383 9400**. It separately lists **SEKORORO clinic**, phone **015 383 9925/26**. The clinic and hospital are distinct listings. [2] [3]
- A 2026 Limpopo Department of Education vacancy document gives Metz Mamahlola Primary School and Ramatau Secondary School addresses as “Metz Village, Ga - Mamahlola, Moetladimo” and also uses Ga-Sekororo in school addresses. This supports Ga-Mamahlola as a locality name associated with Metz/Moetladimo; it does not settle the precise boundary between the names. [4]
- South African Government's Maruleng Thusong Service Centre directory gives an address next to Sekororo Hospital, “Maruleng Central, 75 Metz Village.” [5]
- The South African Post Office's active-sites PDF lists an active **Moetladimo Branch** in Limpopo East, phone **015 791 6053**, postal code **0891**. The reviewed list does not establish an official branch named “Metz Post Office,” and does not provide a street-level address, coordinates or current opening hours. SAPO recommends confirming branch hours directly. Use Moetladimo as the verified listing; treat “Metz Post Office” only as a user-supplied informal landmark name unless SAPO confirms it. [6] [7]
- The Mahlakung Shopping Centre project architect places the retail project in Metz, Limpopo, near Hoedspruit. This is non-government project context, not evidence of a current renovation or official public works announcement. [8]
- The reviewed sources did not provide authoritative coordinates for Ga-Sekororo, Ga-Mamahlola, Mahlakung Shopping Centre, Sekororo Hospital or Moetladimo Branch. Do not label point coordinates as verified. A forecast point must be described as approximate and selected using a verified or clearly qualified location source.

## Implementation guidance

- Keep **Sekororo**, **Ga-Sekororo**, **Ga-Mamahlola**, **Metz** and **Moetladimo** as searchable labels/aliases. Do not assert identical boundaries.
- Keep the hospital and clinic records distinct and attribute contact details to the Limpopo Department of Health.
- Use the municipality's IDP for background, not as a current feed of construction work, jobs or public notices.
- A static link directory is not itself proof that a vacancy, renovation or notice is current. Current claims require successfully retrieved official content and its publication/closing date; otherwise say what could not be verified and link to the source directory.

## Municipal notices and infrastructure sources

- Greater Tzaneen Municipality (GTM) provides dated notice-board entries on its homepage and a dated archived-notices page. The archive contains old service-disruption alerts, so an item must be checked for publication date, affected place, start/end time and later replacement. [9] [10]
- GTM publishes dated road-project and council-planning news. Its Serututung–Tickyline post is dated 2026-08-19 and discusses an anticipated August 2028 completion; that timeline is a forecast from the announcement, not later-confirmed delivery. Other pages include past projected completion dates and must not be called current without a newer official update. [11] [13]
- Mopani District Municipality (MDM) separates notices, latest news, media releases and tender states. Its notice list has no visible date column; inspect linked documents for issue date and location. Tenders show advertised, awarded, archived, cancelled and closure categories, but a tender record does not prove a project is underway or complete. [14] [17]
- Official 2025 MDM documents disagree in scope/status snapshots for Mametja–Sekororo Regional Water Scheme Phase 2A: a September 2025 mayoral bulletin said it had stalled over funding, while the 2025/26 first-quarter report listed R35 million and 21% progress with DWS confirmation pending. Neither verifies status on 2026-10-05; a new MDM/DWS statement is needed before calling it current. [15] [16]
- Limpopo Department of Public Works, Roads and Infrastructure (LDPWRI) exposes News/Notices, events and project cards. The fetched page did not provide a dated detailed schedule for a featured D3642 road-maintenance item. Treat such cards as a lead, not a live traffic closure or service alert. The advertised-tender page has explicit search/pagination and closing dates, but procurement is distinct from construction status. [18] [19]
- The reviewed municipality/province pages showed no discoverable RSS feed. Do not assume the absence of a visible feed means that an undocumented API/feed does not exist; use these direct official pages for discovery and verify each linked notice.

## Public-service vacancies and job-scam safety

- The Limpopo provincial jobs index and official eRecruitment browse page are the first sources for provincial vacancies. The online portal advertises search by title/reference or department. Read the original advert for closing date/time and application channel; hand-delivery and online deadlines can differ. [20] [21]
- GTM has a dedicated current-vacancies page, but it was empty when checked on 2026-10-05. This only describes that page snapshot and does not prove that there are no vacancies elsewhere. Mopani's vacancy page is an advert archive with expired entries, so inspect the direct advert for its deadline. [23] [24]
- DPSA's public-service vacancy circular is weekly except in December. Start with its latest dated issue and verify the relevant Limpopo administration/department PDF; use the advert's own vacancy reference, Z83 instructions and deadline. The gov.za weekly jobs page had segmented PDFs labelled Circular 34 while identifying a complete Circular 35, a mismatch that merits checking before relying on a download. [25] [26] [27]
- The gov.za national-department jobs directory points to departments rather than one complete search database. Some official department portals can have contradictory banners and listing rows. Confirm the job remains open in the original official advert and application portal. [28] [29] [30]
- Official Department of Employment and Labour guidance says work seekers must not be charged for employment services; official government scam warnings describe fake recruitment/appointment-fee requests. The community assistant should advise users to verify employer domains and reference numbers and **never pay to secure a job**. [31] [32]

The vacancy and notice checks above are snapshots dated **2026-10-05**, not current feeds. Do not hard-code a specific job, deadline or project status into the product. Future answers should retrieve the latest relevant official item and identify any date mismatch or stale listing.

## Ga-Sekororo forecast source and limits

- GeoNames identifies Ga-Sekororo (feature ID 1002777) as a populated place in Maruleng, Mopani District, Limpopo and provides representative coordinates **-24.21667, 30.40000**. This is a locality point, not a precise position for each household. The tested Open-Meteo forecast snapped to a grid-cell center about 4.8 km east; display the named locality, not the grid center. [33]
- Open-Meteo's documented `GET /v1/forecast` endpoint returned current and seven-day forecast JSON for that location on 2026-10-05 with `timezone=Africa/Johannesburg`, Celsius, km/h and mm. The response included WMO weather codes, local dates and a grid-center coordinate. [34] [35]
- **Commercial-use limitation:** Open-Meteo's no-key free tier is restricted to non-commercial use, with published request limits and no uptime guarantee. If TMJ is monetized, ad-supported or otherwise commercial under Open-Meteo's stated terms, move to its paid commercial endpoint/API key or another provider before that use. [36] [37]
- Open-Meteo forecast data are CC BY 4.0. The page must show adjacent attribution such as “Weather data by Open-Meteo.com,” link both Open-Meteo and the CC BY 4.0 licence, and state when values have been converted or summaries derived. [38]
- Open-Meteo combines model data from national weather services but is **not the South African Weather Service (SAWS)**. The public SAWS site shows forecast and warning experiences; no public developer-facing, no-key SAWS API was verified in this research. Clearly label the forecast as model guidance, not an official warning, and link users to live SAWS warnings. [39]

The location choice and provider terms are not a guarantee of forecast accuracy or availability. Forecast conditions can vary across the settlement. Recheck provider terms and commercial eligibility before monetizing the app.

## Sepedi, Xitsonga and Tshivenda language-label and capability notes

- South Africa's Constitution names Sepedi, Tshivenda and Xitsonga as official languages. PanSALB's 2026-09-21 resolution uses Sepedi rather than Sesotho sa Leboa in its institutional communications. Northern Sotho/Pedi and Sesotho sa Leboa remain useful aliases when searching source material. [40] [41]
- ISO 639-3 identifiers are `nso` (Pedi/Northern Sotho/Sepedi), `tso` (Tsonga; ISO 639-1 `ts`) and `ven` (Venda; ISO 639-1 `ve`). These are reference metadata; do not assume a particular model API accepts them. [42] [43] [44]
- Cloudflare lists M2M100 as a hosted translation model. Its upstream Meta model card names Northern Sotho as `ns`, but it does not list Tsonga or Venda. Cloudflare's documentation does not clarify whether it accepts `ns`/`nso` or provide model-specific coverage/quality results for the requested languages. Generic multilingual-model descriptions are not evidence of support. No reviewed source establishes quality for a Cloudflare model in Sepedi, Xitsonga or Tshivenda. [45] [46] [47]
- The chat interface has no language selector and defaults to English; users can request translation directly in their message. Translation quality remains unverified, so do not promise accuracy or certification. [48]
